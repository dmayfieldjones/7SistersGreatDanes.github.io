All COI values calculated from full 9-generation pedigree. How to calculate COI

Pedigrees alone can be hard. This tool helps.

Highlight common ancestors, trace shared kennels, and see each ancestor's pedigree-predicted contribution to the cross. Without sequencing the dogs directly, this does not replace genetic analysis — it is a pedigree-based estimate, not a measure of actual shared DNA.

The 10-generation approach was inspired by the 2025 PNAS Special Feature on canine genetics—particularly the German Shepherd genomic time series showing how breeding decisions accumulate across decades. Scarsbrook et al., A 120-y time series of genomes reveals the consequences of closed breeding in German Shepherd Dogs, PNAS (2025)

Key Structural Findings

COI revision: 2.1% → 4.04%. The original estimate of ~2.1% was based on incomplete data in generations 8–9. With complete ancestry through generation 9, the actual Wright's COI is 4.04%. The increase comes primarily from deep foundation dogs — Honey Lane's Highlight alone contributes through 120 separate cross-paths (0.27% total), and Don-Lu's Preferred Risk adds another 40 paths (0.13%). These are the breed's structural bedrock, appearing in nearly every fawn/brindle pedigree.

Parent COI revisions. Piper's own COI went from 0.0% (at AKC depth) to 1.00% with 56 common ancestors — still very low, confirming her as an excellent outcross foundation. Ezra's own COI is 2.50% (48 common ancestors), driven primarily by Nash Bridges Royhl (0.34%) and the Krisda/Sarmac cluster. Both parents are well below breed average.

Still an effective outcross. At 4.04%, this cross remains well below 6.25% (first-cousin equivalent) and far below the breed average of 10–15% at this depth. The structural diversity between Ezra's Scandinavian/Diplomatic's lines and Piper's American Longo/Fendane lines is real and significant.

Diplomatic's Umberto di Visconti (DKCh SCH NCH NordCH) appears 3× internally in Ezra's sire pedigree but traces to the Grand Fawns and Airways kennels in Scandinavia — lines that do not appear at accessible depth in Piper's American pedigree. This remains a genuine source of new genetic diversity.

Avanti's All For One Elan (0.439% via 8 paths) remains the single largest named contributor, entering through both Ezra's dam cluster and Piper's Danekraafts/Elan lines.

The Fendane full-sibling pair — Eb N Prf So You Wanna Play With Magic (Piper) and Winter Is Coming (Ezra) — drive the two largest single-path contributions (Saul's TM and Allie-Gator at 0.39% each).
