All COI values calculated from full 9-generation pedigree. How to calculate COI

Pedigrees alone can be hard. This tool helps.

Highlight common ancestors, trace shared kennels, and see each ancestor's pedigree-predicted contribution to the cross. Without sequencing the dogs directly, this does not replace genetic analysis — it is a pedigree-based estimate, not a measure of actual shared DNA.

The 10-generation approach was inspired by the 2025 PNAS Special Feature on canine genetics—particularly the German Shepherd genomic time series showing how breeding decisions accumulate across decades. Scarsbrook et al., A 120-y time series of genomes reveals the consequences of closed breeding in German Shepherd Dogs, PNAS (2025)

Key Structural Findings

Higher than the Ezra × Piper pairing. This exploratory Ezra × Mia cross computes to 5.42% Wright's COI (135 common ancestors, 821 cross-paths) — about 1.38 points higher than the 4.04% computed for Ezra × Piper. Mia carries all of Piper's ancestry plus all of Rolex's, both verified to a full 9 generations with no gaps. The increase comes from Rolex's side: several of his ancestors — Nash Bridges Royhl, Krisda's Bastet, Avanti's Royhl Rogue, Don-Lu's Preferred Risk, Challenger's Allegra V Avanti, Honey Lane's Highlight, Sarmac's I've Arrived — are independently already common ancestors on Ezra's side too, from the same Fendane/Rojon/Honey Lane cluster of American show lines.

Mia's own COI is meaningfully higher than Piper's. Computed the same way, Mia's own COI is 7.25% (85 common ancestors) versus Ezra's own COI of 1.68% (21 common ancestors). Mia's sire Rolex and dam Piper already share real ancestry, which is inbreeding she carries into any pairing, independent of who she's bred to.

All 1,022 of 1,022 possible ancestor positions are filled and verified — every box on this page, on both sides, traces to a real, named, correctly-sexed dog through 9 generations, with zero sire/dam slot conflicts anywhere in the tree.

Largest contributors: Am Ch Saul's Tequila Mockingbird, Am GCh Fendanesylcrests See You Later Allie-Gator, BISS Am Ch Honey Lane's Highlight lead the common-ancestor list — see the Wright's COI Path Table above for the full top 20.

Methodology & data-provenance note. All figures are computed fresh from the 9-generation grid shown below, using the path-coefficient method validated against the published Ezra × Piper page (reproduced its 4.04% / 123 CA / 618-path figures to within rounding). Rolex's ancestry could not be sourced from the older Rolex × Piper generator page previously published in this repo's history: cross-checking it against breeder-supplied pedigree screens found that old page placed at least two dogs in the wrong ancestor slots (e.g. Piper's own sire appeared as "Rolex's dam"), and its headline stats (10.33% cross-COI, 11.19%/2.19% own-COI) don't reconcile with its own displayed grid — none of that was reused. Rolex's branch here was instead built by walking the breeder's own Quintessa pedigree database (a 118,000+ dog relational file with sire/dam ID references) directly from Rolex out to 9 generations, which is why this branch is complete with no leaves or gaps, unlike the partial reconstruction in earlier drafts of this page.
